WELCOME (30 seconds)

Hello everyone, and welcome to today's class. I'm {author}.

Today we're going to learn about {topic}. By the end of this lesson, you'll be able to answer three questions:

One — the first key question.
Two — the second key question.
Three — the third key question.

Let's get started.

REVIEW (2 minutes)

Before the new material, let's remember what we covered last time. Who can remind us the main idea of the previous lesson?

Give a short recap in your own words. Two or three sentences are enough.

NEW CONTENT — PART 1 (5 minutes)

Here's the first big idea of today.

Explain the concept slowly, with one example. Pause here in the recording — let the idea breathe.

Ask a check question: can you think of another example of this?

NEW CONTENT — PART 2 (5 minutes)

Now that we have the first idea, let's build on it.

This second part is the hardest of the lesson. Go step by step, and use the board or slides here.

PRACTICE (5 minutes)

It's your turn now. Here's a short exercise:

State the exercise clearly. Read it twice if it's long.

I'll give you two minutes. Pause the video and try it before watching the solution.

CLOSING (1 minute)

Let's summarize what we learned today:

First, first key idea.
Second, second key idea.
And third, third key idea.

For next class, please review this lesson and bring one question.

See you next time!
